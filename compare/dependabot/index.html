VibeShield vs Dependabot

What Dependabot does well: GitHub-native update and CVE alerts. Free, and it keeps working with VibeShield on.

Where AI-code risk still falls through: Dependabot waits for an advisory, then opens an update PR. That loop assumes somebody eventually finds and reports the vulnerability. A slopsquatted package is abuse reported zero weeks ago, and the AI-specific problems — pasted secrets, debug-mode scaffolding, injection payloads — aren't dependencies at all. Is Dependabot enough? For its lane: yes, keep it. It just covers one of the seven failure modes.

Capability VibeShield Dependabot
CVE update PRs ❌ out of scope ✅ native, free
Hallucinated packages (no CVE yet) ✅ ❌ nothing to match
Secrets / insecure boilerplate in code ✅ ❌
Transitive tree delta at PR time ✅ per-PR ⚠️ alerts after merge
AI-origin attribution ✅ ❌
Works alongside the other ✅ ✅
